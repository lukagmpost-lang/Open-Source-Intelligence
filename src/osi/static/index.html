Lattice

Ask questions about any chat. Every answer shows its work.

What this is. A tool that reads a chat export (WhatsApp, Slack, Telegram) and answers questions about the people in it.

How it works. When you ask a question, a language model writes Python to explore the data, runs it, sees the results, and iterates until it can answer. Every answer comes with the exact code it ran — expand how this was computed under any answer to see the math.

What you can ask. Who is most central. Who positions people against each other. Who complains the most. Who talks to whom. Follow-ups remember the people mentioned in the previous answer.
